Batch Management

Create production batches here. A batch represents one manufacturing lot. After saving a batch, it becomes available in Create Barcode + QR.

Batch Number Format: LPB-YYMMDD-XXXX
Example: LPB-260913-A7K2
The batch number is generated automatically and must not be manually typed.

Saved Batches

All saved production batches are stored in Supabase and are available for barcode creation.
