1. Introduction

Welcome to Liara Professional™, a brand operated by Liara Group Private Limited. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you visit our website, create an account, place an order or contact us.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Shipping and billing information.
  • Account login-related information.
  • Order and transaction information.
  • Product preferences and shopping activity.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

3. Account Information

If you create an account on our website, information associated with your account may be stored so that we can provide account-related services such as order history, customer support and account management.

4. Orders and Payments

When you place an order, we may collect information necessary to process and fulfil that order, including contact, delivery and order details.

Payment information may be processed through third-party payment service providers. We do not intend to store complete payment card information on our own website servers.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Processing and fulfilling orders.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Managing customer accounts.
  • Providing order and delivery updates.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Preventing fraud, misuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Communicating important service-related information.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain shopping functionality, improve user experience and support website operations.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to operate certain parts of our website, such as hosting, authentication, database services, payment processing, analytics or other technical services.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, comply with applicable legal requirements and resolve disputes.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

Requests can be made by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Liara Group Private Limited.
